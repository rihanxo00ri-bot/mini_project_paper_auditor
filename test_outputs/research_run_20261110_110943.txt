The paper demonstrates a *lab‑based* four‑port MIMO using concentric square‑ring patches and two Complementary Split‑Ring Resonators (CSRRs) etched on the ground plane.  It reports:

* “enhanced isolation of **–22 dB** between patch antenna elements” and “mutual coupling reduced by about **6.5 dB**”【P. 1】.  
* A **2:1 VSWR bandwidth of 75 MHz** centred at 2.45 GHz, improved to ≈80 MHz by adding half‑wave slots【P. 2】【P. 3】.
