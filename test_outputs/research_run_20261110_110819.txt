## What Researchers Discovered

**Page 1 (Abstract):** Researchers designed a novel four-port MIMO antenna system using concentric square ring patch antennas, with all four ports resonating at the 2.4 GHz ISM band. They achieved **−22 dB isolation between patch antenna elements**, and CSRR etched on the ground plane reduced mutual coupling by about **6.5 dB**. The antenna operated with a 2:1 VSWR bandwidth of 75 MHz centered at 2.45 GHz, and showed low envelope correlation (good MIMO diversity performance). These results were validated with a fabricated prototype and measurements (e.g., simulated S-parameters in Fig. 7–8, measured results in Fig. 9).

**Pages 2–3 (Antenna Design & Isolation Structure):** The concentric square rings resonate in different transverse magnetic modes. A pair of complementary split ring resonators (CSRR) etched at 45° on ground corners at strong coupling areas polarizes coupling currents by nearly 180°, so vertically polarized waves of identically-polarized ports P1/P4 (and P2/P3) become out of phase, reducing coupling. Simulated current distributions (Fig. 6) confirm reduced ground currents, yielding isolation enhancement of **6.5 dB**. Slot loading improved bandwidth from 50 MHz to 80 MHz (Fig. 4). Without and with CSRR, simulated S-parameters show coupling P1↔P4 and S23 reduced by 6.5 dB; resonant frequency shift is very small; all four ports resonate at 2.45 GHz with minimum 2:1 VSWR bandwidth of 75 MHz (Fig. 8).

**Page 3 (Results & Discussion):** Measured peak gains were 4 dBi (inner ring) and 3.4 dBi (outer ring); measured efficiency was 84.6% for inner ring ports and ~72.5% for outer ring ports. Far-field patterns (Fig. 10) confirmed polarization diversity (ports P1/P2 perpendicular; P3/P4 perpendicular) and angle diversity (different shapes for fundamental vs. higher order modes).

**Page 4 (MIMO Performance Analysis):** Envelope correlation coefficients computed from far-field radiation patterns stayed **below 0.4 within the operating band** (Fig. 11), ensuring good MIMO performance.

**Page 4 (Conclusion):** The authors conclude that the proposed antenna system achieves:
- All four ports resonate simultaneously at **2.45 GHz**.
- **Isolation ≥ −22 dB** between elements.
- The CSRR technique **reverses coupling-current polarity** on the shared ground without requiring additional metallic parts or complex fabrication.
- **Low envelope correlation** (< 0.4) confirming MIMO diversity performance.

In short, researchers discovered that etching non-periodic subwavelength CSRR structures at 45° corners of the shared ground reverses ground currents by ~180°, giving high isolation (−22 dB, −6.5 dB coupling reduction) and good diversity performance among identically-polarized concentric ring MIMO elements — all at 2.45 GHz with a compact 60×60×1.6 mm³ system.
