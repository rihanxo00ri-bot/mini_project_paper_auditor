**What the researcher discovered**

The paper reports a **novel four‑port MIMO antenna built from two concentric square‑ring microstrip patches** that are simultaneously excited in orthogonal transverse‑magnetic (TM) modes.  
To overcome the high mutual coupling that occurs when such closely spaced elements are placed on a single substrate, the authors **load the ground plane between the rings with complementary split‑ring resonators (CSRRs)**.  

Key findings (with page references):

| Discovery | Evidence from the paper |
|---|---|
| **Compact, collocated four‑port MIMO** – all four ports resonate at 2.45 GHz and fit into a 60 × 60 mm × 1.6 mm³ volume. | “more compact than the conventional λ/2 rectangular patch… overall system dimension of 60 x 60 x 1.6 mm³” – **p. 2** |
| **CSRR‑based isolation** – placing two CSRRs on the corners of the rings reverses the polarity of the ground‑plane currents, making vertically polarized waves of adjacent ports out of phase and thereby suppressing coupling. | “CSRR exhibits a resonant property … changes the polarity of coupling current on common shared ground so as to improve isolation… vertically polarized space waves of P1 and P4 are out of phase now which ensures least coupling” – **p. 3** |
| **Isolation improvement** – coupling between identically polarized elements (e.g., P1–P4, S₂₃) is reduced by **≈ 6.5 dB**, giving a required **≥ –22 dB** isolation. | “The coupling between identically polarized elements P1 and P4 is reduced by 6.5 dB with the isolation structure. Same is the case with S23…” – **p. 4** |
| **Bandwidth** – 2:1 VSWR bandwidth of **75 MHz** centered at 2.45 GHz (about 40 % wider than the bare ring antenna). Half‑wave slots further enhance it to 80 MHz. | “operates with a 2:1 VSWR bandwidth of 75 MHz… Slit loading also enhances bandwidth… from 50 MHz to 80 MHz” – **p. 2** / **p. 4** |
| **Low inter‑element correlation** – envelope correlation coefficients stay **below 0.4** across the operating band, guaranteeing good MIMO diversity. | “within the operating band correlation coefficients are below 0.4 which ensures good MIMO performance.” – **p. 5** |

| **Angle/polarization diversity** – different radiation patterns for fundamental and higher‑order modes give distinct azimuthal/angular behavior, providing angle diversity. | “Measured normalized radiation pattern… shows that feed ports P1 and P2 have polarization planes perpendicular to each other… same polarization diversity can also be observed between ring antenna ports P3 and P4… guarantees angle diversity.” – **p. 4** |

### Summary of the core contribution
The researchers **discovered a practical, sub‑wavelength, four‑port MIMO system** in which **complementary split‑ring resonators etched on the ground plane between concentric square‑ring patches reverse the ground‑current polarity and suppress mutual coupling**. This yields:
* At least **–22 dB isolation** between adjacent ports,
* A **wide 75 MHz (2:1 VSWR) bandwidth** around 2.45 GHz,
* **Low envelope correlation (<0.4)**, i.e., strong diversity,
* High gain (≈ 4 dBi inner, ≈ 3.4 dBi outer) and efficiency (> 70 %), and
* A **compact footprint** suitable for portable/wireless applications.

These results demonstrate that CSRR‑loaded ground planes can effectively decouple tightly packed MIMO elements, overcoming the usual trade‑off between size and performance.
