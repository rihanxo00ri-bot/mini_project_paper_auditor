### 1. Resonance and Bandwidth
- **Discovery:** The four‑port concentric square‑ring antenna system resonates at **2.45 GHz** with a **2:1 VSWR bandwidth of 75 MHz** (centered at 2.45 GHz).  
- **Evidence:** Abstract, **Page 1** – “All four ports are made to resonate at 2.4 GHz ISM band … The antenna operates with a 2:1 VSWR bandwidth of 75 MHz centered at 2.45 GHz.”

### 2. Isolation and Mutual‑Coupling Reduction via CSRR
- **Discovery:** Loading the ground plane with **complementary split‑ring resonators (CSRRs)** provides an **isolation of at least –22 dB** between the antenna elements and **reduces mutual coupling by ≈ 6.5 dB**.  
- **Evidence:**  
  - Abstract, **Page 1** – “This design uses complementary split ring resonator (CSRR) loaded on its ground plane for enhanced isolation of –22 dB between patch antenna elements. CSRR reduces mutual coupling by about 6.5 dB.”  
  - Section III, **Page 2‑3** – “CSRR reduces mutual coupling by about 6.5 dB” and “isolation enhancement of 6.5 dB” observed in simulated S‑parameters.  
  - Measurement results, **Page 4** – “Measured S parameters … reasonable agreement between simulated and measured results” confirming the –22 dB isolation.

### 3. Low Envelope Correlation (Good MIMO Diversity)
- **Discovery:** The **envelope correlation coefficients remain below 0.4 across the operating band**, indicating good MIMO diversity performance.  
- **Evidence:** Section V, **Page 4** – “It is observed that within the operating band correlation coefficients are below 0.4 which ensures good MIMO performance.”

### 4. Radiation Performance (Gain, Efficiency, and Diversity)
- **Discovery:**  
  - Measured **peak gains**: inner‑ring ports **4 dBi**, outer‑ring ports **3.4 dBi** (ISM band).  
  - Measured **radiation efficiencies**: inner‑ring ports **84.6 %**, outer‑ring ports **≈ 72.5 %**.  
  - Far‑field patterns show **polarization diversity** (P1/P2 orthogonal, P3/P4 orthogonal) and **angle diversity** (different patterns for fundamental vs. higher‑order modes).  
- **Evidence:** Section IV, **Page 4** – “Measured peak gain values of inner ring modes and outer ring modes are 4 dBi and 3.4 dBi respectively. Peak efficiency obtained for inner ring ports are 84.6 %, and outer ring ports are about 72.5 % … Far field radiation pattern measurements … shows that feed ports P1 and P2 have polarization planes perpendicular to each other … The shape of the pattern is different for fundamental and higher order modes … which guarantees angle diversity.”

### 5. Physical‑Size and Integration Advantage
- **Discovery:** The **overall system dimension is compact (60 × 60 × 1.6 mm³)**, achieved by concentric square‑ring patches and sub‑wavelength CSRR structures, with **no additional metallic parts** or intricate fabrication steps.  
- **Evidence:** Abstract, **Page 1** – “All four ports are made to resonate at 2.4 GHz ISM band with an overall system dimension of 60 x 60 x 1.6 mm3.” Section VI, **Page 4** – “The proposed antenna system has simple structure and the isolation technique employed does not include any additional metallic parts or require any intricate fabrication.”
