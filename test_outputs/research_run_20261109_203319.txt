**What the researcher discovered**

The authors created a **compact four‑port MIMO antenna** made from **concentric square‑ring microstrip patches** that are simultaneously excited in *orthogonal higher‑order transverse‑magnetic (TM) modes*.  
To suppress the strong mutual coupling between the closely spaced elements they etch **two complementary split‑ring resonators (CSRRs)** on the ground plane at the corners of the rings (tilted ≈ 45°). The CSRRs interact with the incident magnetic field, disturb the ground‑plane currents on neighboring rings by roughly **180°**, and therefore cause destructive interference of the coupled fields.
